How to Use OED Reader

▼ Getting Started

1. Position your micro-print OED page under good lighting

2. Tap "Scan Entry" and allow camera access

3. Frame the word entry clearly and tap Capture

4. The OCR will process the image and extract the entry

▼ Understanding Entries

Headword: The main word being defined

Etymology: Origin and historical development

Senses: Different meanings of the word

Quotations: Historical usage examples

▼ Tips for Best Results

• Use natural or bright artificial lighting

• Keep the camera steady and focused

• Avoid shadows and glare on the page

• Position the entry in the center of the frame

• Save entries to your collection for later reference
